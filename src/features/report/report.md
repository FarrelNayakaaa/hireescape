> **Owner: Dev A** · Depends on: `features/analyst`, `features/session` (H8), `features/proctoring` (H10, Tier 3) · Consumes recorded sessions; never writes them.

> Inherits the root `AGENTS.md` and `src/components/ui/AGENTS.md`. The report is what the hiring
> manager actually buys. It must be **readable in 2 minutes and verifiable in 5**. It is a sober,
> professional document: no gamification here.

## Replay

- Scrubber over `session.snapshots`; event markers on the scrubber coloured by type
  (test_run green/red, ai_apply purple, inquisitor blue, answers white).
- Editor shows the snapshot code read-only. Toggle "Show origin": tints lines by `Origin`
  (ai = purple 12% alpha, ai_edited = purple outline, manual = none, paste = yellow outline).
  Origin tints are reconstructed by replaying `applyWithProvenance` up to that snapshot.
- Clicking an `EvidenceChip`:
  - `code_line` → jump to the final snapshot, scroll to the line, highlight 2s.
  - `event` → jump to that event's snapshot, highlight the event in the list.
- Keyboard: ←/→ previous/next event, Space play/pause (2× speed, skips idle gaps > 10s).

## Evidence score (section 02)

The score comes from `report.scores`, produced by `deriveScores()` in `features/analyst`
(formula: `src/features/analyst/analyst.md` → "`deriveScores()`"). The report **only renders** it; it
never computes, adjusts, or rounds differently.

- **Objective by construction:** the model never outputs a number. Points come from executed metrics
  (tests, timers, provenance) and from the *count* of verified claims. Unverified claims are already
  dropped, so they cannot add or remove points.
- **Always show the breakdown**: every dimension expands to show each input, its value, and its points
  (e.g. "Hidden tests 6/6 → 50 / 50"). A score without its breakdown is never rendered.
- **Evidence size next to the number**: "based on N verified claims + M executed metrics". If a
  dimension has fewer than 2 verified claims, show a "Low evidence" tag next to it (text, not colour only).
- **AI OFF:** AI supervision shows "Not assessed — AI assistance was off" (no bar, no 0). The total is
  re-weighted over the assessed dimensions and says so ("total over 3 dimensions").
- **Analysis failed:** show objective-only points under "Partial score (executed metrics only)" and no total.
- **Integrity signals never affect the score** (same isolation as competency levels).
- Neutral styling: one accent colour for all bars; no red/green thresholds, no "pass" line, no grade
  letters, no confetti.
- The score is **per candidate**. Do not sort, rank, or compare candidates by it anywhere (including
  `/manager`); no percentiles.
- Footer copy under the card: "Computed by a fixed formula from executed metrics and verified claims.
  The AI never chooses a number. The score summarises evidence; it is not a hiring decision."

## Labels & honesty rules

- Objective cards carry the label "executed".
- Any claim with `basis: "ai_predicted"` carries `<BasisLabel>` "AI-predicted, not executed".
- When AI was OFF: AI share cards show "AI assistance was off" instead of 0%.
- The only score shown is the evidence score in section 02, always with its breakdown. Never show a
  rank, a percentile, a grade letter, or a pass/fail badge.
- Decision panel copy: "You make the decision. The evidence above is here to help, not to decide."
